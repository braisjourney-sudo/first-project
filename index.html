Brais Fernández Rodríguez

Hello, my name is Brais, and I am originally from the Canary Islands in Spain. I am currently studying Software Engineering at VIA University College. Before moving to Denmark, I finished high school at El Colegio de Santa María Claret. I came to Denmark because I believe the education system here is excellent and offers many more opportunities. Back in Spain, I lived in a family of four with my parents, my younger sister, and our dog. I chose Software Engineering because I felt it was the career that suited me best. I have had a strong interest in technology since I was a child, and I want to pursue it by finishing my studies and building a professional career in the field.
